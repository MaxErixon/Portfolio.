03 / ABOUT

A bit about me.

MAX ERIXON / MALMÖ

I'm Max Erixon. I have a bachelor's degree in Media Technology from Malmö University and work with graphic design, digital content and print materials.

At Moneybear, I manage the WordPress website and make graphics for campaigns, newsletters and customer communication. At Camping Bar, I've designed posters, menus and signage alongside my job as shift manager.

I work in InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator, PowerPoint and Canva, and also use Figma and AI tools in my digital workflow. I'm used to working with existing logos, colours and templates — and to adjusting a design for the format it needs to fit.
