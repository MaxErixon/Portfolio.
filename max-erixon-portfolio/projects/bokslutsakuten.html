ROLE

SCOPE

Website / Social ad / Five-slide carousel

CONTEXT

Moneybear side project

01 / OVERVIEW

A website and matching graphics.

A small website and a set of social graphics for Moneybear’s Bokslutsakuten service.

I built the Bokslutsakuten website as a side project for Moneybear. It explains the service and gives visitors a simple way to ask for help with their accounts.

I also made a social advert and a five-slide Instagram carousel, keeping the same colours and type across the website and posts.

02 / SOCIAL AD

Social advert.

A vertical Instagram advert with a clear headline, a short list of benefits and a button-style call to action.

CAMPAIGN / GRAPHIC DESIGN

Making the message clear.

A direct headline, benefit-led content and a contrasting call-to-action button guide the viewer through the message.

The visual design adapts the brand’s typographic system and blue-and-orange color palette for a fast-moving social feed.
