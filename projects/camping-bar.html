Camping Bar

Print, event & digital design

ROLE

Shift manager · Graphic materials alongside my role

SCOPE

Signage / menus / event posters / Instagram

CONTEXT

Camping Bar · Malmö, Sweden

01 / OVERVIEW

Made for the venue.

A selection of visual communication created for Camping Bar in Malmö, from large-format printed signage and a festival bar menu to quiz promotions for print and Instagram.

These are materials I created for Camping Bar in Malmö: entrance signage, a festival bar menu, a quiz poster and Instagram promotion. The printed pieces were made for guests to read at the venue.

The aim was to make prices, activities and event information quick to find, while staying close to Camping's visual style.

02 / PRINT

Posters & signage.

Original print designs shown without mockups, so layout, information hierarchy and typography can be viewed clearly. Select an image to open it at full size.

03 / DIGITAL

Event design for social.

A vertical Instagram-format graphic promoting the weekly trivia quiz, with event details, prizes and a booking link presented in a mobile-first layout.

SOCIAL / EVENT PROMOTION

A quiz night worth sharing.

This Instagram post promotes the weekly quiz at Camping. The time and prize details are prominent, with the booking link and QR code lower down.

The print poster uses the same look in a format people can read at the venue.
